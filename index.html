Signing In

How Do I Get an Account?

The ALE app is for adviser firms that work with Adviser Lead Engine. Your firm's account manager invites you by email. Open the link in that email and choose a password. Then open the app, tap Sign In, and enter your email and that password.

I Did Not Get My Invite

Check your junk or spam folder. Invite links work once and last seven days, so if yours has expired, ask your account manager to send a new one.

It Says My Email or Password Is Wrong

Use the exact address your invite was sent to. If you have forgotten your password, tap Forgot Password on the sign in screen. If it still does not work, ask your account manager to check your invite, or email us.

I Forgot My Password

On the sign in screen, tap Forgot Password and enter your email. We will send you a link to choose a new password. The link works once and lasts an hour.

Using the App

I Cannot See the Leads Tab

Your firm's account manager controls which sections your firm can see. Email us and we will check your firm's settings.

Calling a Lead

Open a lead and tap WhatsApp Call or Call. The app opens WhatsApp or your phone. After the call, set the lead's stage to New, Qualified, Booked, Won or Lost, and add a note if it helps your team.

Your Account and Data

Deleting Your Account

Tap your profile in the top right, open Settings, then Delete Account. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.
